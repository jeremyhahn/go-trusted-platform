## Purpose

go-trusted-platform is a Trusted Computing software platform: the trusted-computing platform product and control plane for provisioning, node and workload attestation (Attester and Relying Party), SDN, the `gatewayd` edge gateway, the attestation-gated OCI registry, DNS, tenancy and fleet operations.
It is layer 5 of the stack, one product in layers ([02 §The stack](../../handbook/software/docs/02-dependency-graph.md#the-stack-one-product-in-layers)): it embeds go-quicbaas as a library, and only go-quicbaas, and inherits all of its functionality.
Its SDK, `sdk/go`, re-exports every go-quicbaas service, and through go-quicbaas the go-qrdb database services and the go-xkms hardware crypto services, under their own names; the platform adds its platform services on top ([00 L5](../../handbook/software/docs/00-ownership-and-sdk-boundary.md#l5-every-layer-re-exports-every-lower-service)).
Its consumers are OEM device manufacturers, enterprise administrators, platform administrators and end users; it may be used as a program, toolkit or library for building platforms and services on trusted and confidential computing.

## Boundaries

A deployment has one cluster engine and one datastore, go-qrdb's, reached through go-quicbaas; the platform uses the layers below as a library, never as a second product.

The platform owns:

- the platform services: TPM bring-up, IDevID, attestation, SDN, DNS, BMC, ISO, initrd orchestration, the installer and the daemon;
- its records in the deployment's one datastore: system-level records in the system tenant, each tenant's data in that tenant;
- the exposure of every service on REST, gRPC, QUIC, MCP and the Unix socket, with feature parity across them.

The platform never:

- imports `go-xkms/pkg/*`, `go-xkms/sdk/go/*`, `go-qrdb`, `go-quicraft` or `go-truststrap`; the one upstream path is the `go-quicbaas/sdk/go` re-export;
- consumes a go-qrdb capability, the storage API and object storage included, other than through the go-quicbaas SDK, or requires `go-qrdb` or `go-objstore` directly in a `go.mod` (they appear only as `// indirect` requires of go-quicbaas); only go-xkms and go-quicbaas use go-qrdb directly, and go-quicbaas adds the go-xkms hardware crypto at go-qrdb's seams (owner ruling 228 (2), 2026-10-27; the layered stack ruling of 2026-10-14);
- runs a second database, cluster or engine, or keeps a store, service or client of its own for something a lower layer provides: its keys, certificates, CA, LUKS sealer and signing backends are the go-xkms services re-exported through the go-quicbaas SDK (`sdk/go/xkms/*`), used as a library;
- shares a client across tenants; tenants are strictly isolated;
- re-implements a go-quicbaas surface or anything the SDK exposes (FIDO2, WebAuthn, barrier, ceremony, trust-bootstrap dispatch, TPM sealing); a feature it needs from below is a seam row in this SPEC and in the lower project's SPEC and a re-export in `sdk/go`, never a parallel implementation, and a missing capability means the SDK is extended, not bypassed;
- wraps, filters or re-models a lower layer's service under its own name; it adds services;
- composes go-xkms implementations into go-qrdb seams (`StrategyRegistry`, `PINBackend`, `FactorRegistry`, `CeremonyCA`) or hardware crypto implementations into go-xkms, or blank-imports a device adapter such as trusted-firmware's `xkms`; that composition is go-quicbaas's alone, and the platform reaches devices through the go-quicbaas SDK;
- ships a "wire this up later" stub on a transport (`NotImplementedError`, `BaasTransportNotSupportedError`, `not yet supported by SDK`);
- builds the P2P network beyond design.

- SDK: Go, `sdk/go`, [docs/sdk/architecture.md](sdk/architecture.md); JavaScript, `trusted-platform-sdk`, [docs/api/sdk-javascript.md](api/sdk-javascript.md).
- CLI and binaries: `platformd`, `platformctl` (CLI and desktop GUI), `pxeboot`, `platform-initrd`, `deployer-pin`, [docs/daemon/cli.md](daemon/cli.md); `gatewayd`, [docs/gateway/cli.md](gateway/cli.md).
- API transports: Embedded, Unix, gRPC, REST, QUIC, MCP, [docs/daemon/api.md](daemon/api.md) (canonical).
- Web UI: the control panel served by `platformd`, [docs/web/architecture.md](web/architecture.md).
- go-quicbaas SDK consumed for unattended unlock (#720, go-xkms #1443): `Security().TPMPolicy()` create, update, update `--branch next --reference-values` and verify (not yet published); the `Security().Attestation()` reference-values verbs `PutReferenceValues`, `GetReferenceValues`, `ListReferenceValues` and `DeleteReferenceValues` (published in go-quicbaas v0.14.0-alpha, not in the pinned v0.12.0-alpha); in `sdk/go/xkms`, `NewStaticPolicyBindingResolver`, the binding-record decode and `SetPolicyBindingResolver` on `TrustedPlatformModule` (not yet published).
- go-quicbaas SDK consumed for FIDO2 PINs, PIV signing and key import, and assertion verification (#584; luks, installer, vpn, auth): in `sdk/go/xkms/fido2`, the PIN-carrying FIDO2 assertion and its PIN errors, for the luks FIDO2 keyslot, the initrd FIDO2 unlock and the vpn `fido2` person-factor signer when a key requires its PIN (today only the PIN-less `NewHandler` is re-exported); the go-xkms smart-card PIV key generator composed by go-quicbaas behind `SetPIVBackendResolver`, so `SignPIV` (`sdk/go/xkms`) signs on a physical card for the platformd PIV service, PIV login and the vpn `piv` signer; go-xkms `ImportPIVKey` re-exported in `sdk/go/xkms`, so the platformd PIV service imports a private key into a slot (today `pkg/app/backends/piv.go:229` fails closed with `ErrPIVKeyImportUnsupported`); and a `Security().Auth().FIDO2()` verb, composed from a go-xkms `webauthn` primitive, that verifies a WebAuthn assertion over a challenge the caller supplies, for the vpn gateway's `FactorVerifier` (not published in go-quicbaas v0.15.0-alpha; the platform pins v0.12.0-alpha).
- go-quicbaas SDK consumed for IAM role assignment and group membership on the embedded transport (#949; iam, tenant): embedded-transport handlers for the `client/iam` verbs `quicbaas.IAM.User/AssignRole`, `quicbaas.IAM.User/RevokeRole`, `quicbaas.IAM.User/AddToGroup`, `quicbaas.IAM.User/RemoveFromGroup`, `quicbaas.IAM.Group/AddMember`, `quicbaas.IAM.Group/RemoveMember` and `quicbaas.IAM.Group/ListMembers`. The embedded dispatch table registers none of them (`transport/embedded/iam_dispatch.go:37-118`, v0.12.0-alpha), so platformd's IAM services fail them on every transport when the daemon runs on the embedded SDK (`pkg/api/daemon/iam_grpc_service.go:634`, `:675`, `:928`, `:970`), and so do the IAM and tenant integration suites (`test/integration/iam`, `test/integration/tenant`). v0.14.0-alpha registers none of them either, and drops the `quicbaas.IAM.Group` handlers and `GroupClient` altogether (not published in v0.12.0-alpha or v0.14.0-alpha; the platform pins v0.12.0-alpha; go-quicbaas row: m512, consumer gtp; go-quicbaas issue to be filed after both rows merge (m513)).
- NBD volumes behind a VolumeProvider (#689): the NBD-over-TLS export server (`pkg/bootstrap/nbd`) reaches its per-device volumes only through a VolumeProvider, and the server itself is unchanged by the choice of store. The provider gives the server five operations: create (on first attach, at the configured size), grow-only resize (grow to a larger configured size, never shrink), attach to a device path (the boot node's own read of the UKI from the volume goes through the same attach), promote and demote (the server serves a volume only while its replica is promoted). There are two stores: local files on a single boot node (today's store), and go-blockstore's DRBD provider on the gateway pair edge-01/edge-02, which replicates each device's volume between the gateways. go-blockstore is consumed through the go-quicbaas SDK, whose orchestration-volumes row composes it; the platform does not import go-blockstore. The NBD server certificate is issued by the bootstrap CA (the go-xkms CA re-exported through the go-quicbaas SDK, `sdk/go/xkms/ca`) to a dedicated role that holds `certs:generate` for the NBD export server's service identity only. Open with go-blockstore: who decides promote and demote (proposed: the platform's gateway HA, #710, with the provider only performing them); whether go-blockstore defines the VolumeProvider interface that the platform's local-file store implements; the size units (bytes) and when a resize happens (at the next attach, never under an attached client).
- IAM clean-up: go-qrdb is the one source of roles, permissions and authorization decisions, reached through the go-quicbaas SDK. Each row below names a place where the platform still keeps its own, and gives either the re-export that replaces it or the SDK gap it waits on. Device onboarding (`device:write`, `provisioning:read`, `provisioning:write`, `idevid:*`) and OTA activate and confirm are permissions in go-qrdb's vocabulary. The mTLS gRPC interceptors authenticate but run no per-method check (`pkg/api/daemon/mtls_interceptor.go`). QUIC allows every request when no checker is wired, and every opcode with an empty scope (`pkg/api/quic/auth.go`). Replacement: `IAMPEPClient.Check` (`sdk/go`, alias of `client/iam.PEPClient`) decides every request on every transport for the authenticated principal. A request is denied when no decision point is wired, or when its operation has no permission.
- IAM clean-up, auth-factor administration: `callerAuthorized` (`pkg/api/rest/auth_factor_handlers.go`) admits three callers: one with no TLS peer, one whose certificate CN starts with `admin`, and one whose OU is `admin`. The subject OU names the tenant, so that check reads a tenant name as a role. Replacement: `IAMPEPClient.Check` on go-qrdb's auth-factor permissions for the session principal. A caller with no authenticated principal is refused.
- IAM clean-up, Unix socket root: the Unix gRPC server chains only the recovery and peer-credential interceptors, with no per-method check (`pkg/api/daemon/daemon.go`), and CA CSR signing admits a peer-credential root (`pkg/api/grpc/ca_service.go`). - IAM clean-up, local role gates: `gated(common.ROLE_ADMIN)` on the web routes (`pkg/webservice/v1/router.go`, `pkg/webservice/v1/authz.go`), `RequireRoles`, `RequireAllRoles`, `HasRole` and `RequireRole` (`pkg/webservice/v1/middleware/iam/`), and the route scope guard, which passes when no authorizer is wired (`pkg/webservice/v1/router.go`). Replacement: `platformtoken.AuthorizedContext.RequireScope` and `RequireAnyScope` (`sdk/go/platformtoken`) on each route's permission, as the platform's `RequireScope` already does. A route is refused when no authorizer is wired.
- IAM clean-up, local role store and assignment: the `Role` entity, the user's `Roles`, `AddRole` and `HasRole` (`pkg/store/datastore/entities/`), the role DAOs (`pkg/store/datastore/kvstore/role.go`, `pkg/storage/quicbaas/role.go`) and the role service (`pkg/platform/service/role.go`), the invitation role list (`pkg/webservice/v1/invitation/types.go`), and the admin claim's local `ROLE_ADMIN` grant (`pkg/webservice/v1/adminclaim/service.go`). Replacement: `rbac.Client` `ListRoles` and `AssignRole` (`sdk/go/client/rbac`) and `IAMRoleClient.Reconcile` (`sdk/go`, alias of `client/iam.RoleClient`).
- IAM clean-up, tenant owner: tenant ownership is a user field, a role the platform assigns itself (`admin`, plus a synthesized `tenant_owner`) (`pkg/platform/service/tenant_user.go`), a token claim (`pkg/webservice/v1/jwt/`, `pkg/webservice/v1/middleware/tenant/context.go`) and the invitation gate (`pkg/webservice/v1/invitation/handler.go`). Replacement: ownership becomes a go-qrdb role assignment made through `rbac.Client.AssignRole` and decided by `IAMPEPClient.Check`. It is not a user field or a token claim.
- IAM clean-up, local role names: `ROLE_SO` and `ROLE_ADMIN` (`pkg/common/types.go`), the FIDO2 roles (`pkg/api/daemon/fido2_service.go`) and the ceremony officer roles (`pkg/api/daemon/ceremony_service.go`). SDK gap: a go-quicbaas re-export of go-qrdb's built-in role names (`BuiltInRoles` in go-qrdb `sdk/go`), and of the ceremony and FIDO2 officer roles as go-qrdb roles or permissions.
- IAM clean-up, MCP tool permissions: the MCP tool permission catalogue and its own `resource:action` parsing (`pkg/api/mcp/authz.go`). Replacement: `ModuleMCPToolPermissions` (`sdk/go`, go-quicbaas API module) declares each tool's `Permission`, and `IAMPEPClient.Check` decides it.
- IAM clean-up, gRPC and QUIC permission maps: the gRPC method map (`pkg/api/grpc/method_permissions.go`) and the QUIC opcode catalogue (`pkg/api/quic/opcode_permissions.go`). SDK gap: an API-module declaration of per-method gRPC permissions and per-opcode QUIC permissions, in the same form as `ModuleMCPToolPermissions` (today `ModuleGRPCPublicMethods` lists only the public methods).

SDK gap: `ca:sign-csr` as a go-qrdb permission carried by a default role and re-exported in go-quicbaas `sdk/go`.
- IAM clean-up, onboarding permissions: `device:write`, `provisioning:read`, `provisioning:write` and `idevid:read`, `idevid:write`, `idevid:delete` are declared only in the platform's catalogues (`pkg/api/grpc/method_permissions.go`, `pkg/api/quic/opcode_permissions.go`, `pkg/api/mcp/authz.go`, `pkg/webservice/v1/router/device.go`). SDK gap: these permissions in go-qrdb's vocabulary, carried by its default roles and re-exported in go-quicbaas `sdk/go`; never roles of their own.
- IAM clean-up, tenant scope: own-tenant-or-system checks (`pkg/tenant/access.go`, `pkg/api/daemon/tenant_grpc_authz.go`), `RequireTenant`, `RequireOwnTenantOrSystem` and `RequireOwnTenantNameOrSystem` (`pkg/webservice/v1/middleware/iam/guard.go`), and the MCP invitation tools, which cannot express the system-tenant requirement (`pkg/api/mcp/authz.go`). Replacement: `IAMDomainTenantManager` (`sdk/go`, go-quicbaas IAM domain) with `IAMPEPClient.Check` scoped to the request's tenant. Open with go-quicbaas: does the tenant manager cover the system tenant acting on any tenant?
- IAM clean-up, cross-tenant user administration: `RequireOwnTenant` (`pkg/webservice/v1/middleware/iam/guard.go`, mounted in `pkg/webservice/v1/router.go` and `pkg/webservice/v1/rest/iam/router.go`) and the IAM tenant check (`pkg/api/daemon/iam_tenant_authz.go`) refuse a system-tenant Admin on another tenant's users. SDK gap: go-qrdb #683, under which a system-tenant Admin can administer a tenant's users and the decision point enforces it. The local mitigation stays until go-qrdb #683 lands.
- IAM clean-up, tenant from the certificate organization (#926): the mTLS adapter reads the tenant from a configurable subject field (O by default, with no OU option), falls back to O when that field is empty, and substitutes a configured default tenant when none is found (`pkg/auth/mtls.go`, `pkg/auth/interface.go`). The daemon's mTLS transports use this adapter (`pkg/api/daemon/transport_auth.go`) and so does gatewayd (`pkg/sdn/gateway/commands/assembly.go`). The SDN certificate policy reads O and then the first CN component (`pkg/sdn/policy/certificate.go`), and the enrollment port policy reads O, else `default` (`pkg/sdn/policy/enrollment_hook.go`). Replacement: the tenant is the subject OU of the verified principal or device certificate, on every path. A certificate with no OU has no tenant and is refused. SDK gap: see the IAM seam row below (tenant resolution from a verified certificate).
- IAM clean-up, caller-chosen and default tenants (#926): the webservice auth middleware takes the tenant from the token claim, then from the caller's `X-Tenant-ID` header, then from a configured `DefaultTenantID`. It reads a certificate's tenant from a configurable CN, OU, O, SAN email domain or SAN DNS name (`pkg/webservice/v1/middleware/iam/auth.go`, `pkg/webservice/v1/middleware/iam/types.go`). Its `NewAuthMiddleware` has no production caller and goes with the rest. The MCP auth adapter falls back to a configured `defaultTenantID` when a token names no tenant (`pkg/api/mcp/dragondb_auth.go`, wired in `pkg/api/daemon/daemon.go`). Replacement: the tenant comes only from the authenticated principal, whose tenant is its certificate's subject OU, and is passed to `IAMPEPClient.Check` as `CheckRequest.TenantID`. A header never names the tenant. A request with no principal tenant is refused and never defaulted.
- IAM clean-up, admin-claim tenant from a SPIFFE URI (#926): the admin claim proves the caller's tenant by matching the configured operating tenant against the URI SAN path `spiffe://<domain>/tenant/<tenant>/user/<admin>` (`pkg/webservice/v1/adminclaim/proof.go`), not against the certificate's subject OU. Replacement: the tenant is the subject OU of the verified admin certificate, and the claim's role grant goes through `rbac.Client.AssignRole` (see "local role store and assignment").
- IAM clean-up, request-named tenants with no tenant check (#926): the VPN service manages peers and gateways for a tenant named in the request, and lists every tenant's peers when none is named, under only `vpn:read`, `vpn:write` and `vpn:admin` (`pkg/api/daemon/vpn_grpc_service.go`, `pkg/api/rest/vpn_handlers.go`, `pkg/webservice/v1/rest/vpn/router.go`). EAB key administration (`pkg/api/daemon/eab_grpc_service.go`, `pkg/webservice/v1/rest/eab/handlers.go`), gRPC invitation create and list (`pkg/api/daemon/invitation_grpc_service.go`) and device templates (`pkg/webservice/v1/device/template_handlers.go`) do the same: none of them compares the named tenant with the caller's. Replacement: `IAMPEPClient.Check` with `CheckRequest.TenantID` set to the request's tenant and the subject's tenant set to the caller's OU tenant, so that go-qrdb decides cross-tenant access through `IAMDomainTenantManager` (see "tenant scope").
- IAM clean-up, proxy route roles (#926): proxy routes carry `required-roles` beside `required-scopes` (`pkg/proxy/config.go`, also set through `pkg/api/daemon/proxy_store.go`, `pkg/sdn/gateway/proxysvc/config.go` and `pkg/cli/commands/proxy/route.go`). `hasAllRoles` checks them against the token principal's roles (`pkg/proxy/tokenauth.go`) and against the certificate identity's `roles` attribute, which falls back to the subject OU (`pkg/proxy/authchain.go`), so a tenant name is read as a role. Roles also feed the OPA `proxy_authz` input (`pkg/proxy/policy.go`, `pkg/policy/opa/policies/proxy_authz.rego`). Replacement: `required-roles` goes, and a route's permission is `required-scopes`, decided by `IAMPEPClient.Check` for the authenticated principal. The OPA route policy keeps its zone, attestation and method rules but takes no roles.
- IAM clean-up, registry access policy (#926): the registry auth handler evaluates an OPA policy, `registry.access`, and grants session permissions from its decision (`pkg/registry/auth.go`, `pkg/registry/registry.go`). The handler has no production caller, and no `registry.access` policy is embedded. Replacement: registry pull and push are decided by `IAMPEPClient.Check`. SDK gap: registry permissions in go-qrdb's vocabulary, carried by its default roles and re-exported in go-quicbaas `sdk/go`.
- IAM clean-up, DNS zone ownership (#926): each zone records an owning tenant (`pkg/dns/entities/zone.go`), and the DNS service admits zone access when that field equals the daemon's configured tenant (`pkg/dns/service.go`, from `pkg/app/app.go`), not the caller's tenant. Replacement: `IAMPEPClient.Check` on the DNS permission, with `CheckRequest.TenantID` set to the zone's tenant and the subject's tenant set to the caller's OU tenant.
- IAM clean-up, local user store (#926, #644): the platform keeps its own user records, with password, tenant and tenant-owner fields (`pkg/store/datastore/entities/user.go`, `pkg/store/datastore/kvstore/user.go`, `pkg/storage/quicbaas/user.go`, `pkg/platform/service/user.go`), and mirrors them into go-quicbaas IAM (`pkg/platform/service/tenant_user.go`, `pkg/platform/service/iam_user_reconcile.go`). Replacement: `IAMUserClient` (`sdk/go`, alias of `client/iam.UserClient`) holds users. The platform keeps no user, password or tenant-owner record of its own, and the mirror goes.
- IAM clean-up, empty permission allowed (#926): the platform token validator's `CheckPermission` allows a request whose resource or action is empty (`pkg/api/daemon/platform_token_validator.go`). Replacement: `IAMPEPClient.Check`. An empty permission is denied, as for the transport checks above.
