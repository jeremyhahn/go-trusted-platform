# Plan #1976: Go test jobs run with TMPDIR=/tmp (main-fix)

## Objective

Main push CI is red in Race and FIPS shards: the self-hosted runner exports
`TMPDIR=/home/runner/_work/_temp`, so unix sockets under `t.TempDir()` get
paths over the 108-byte `sun_path` limit and `bind` fails with
`invalid argument`.

## Slices

1. `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: add `TMPDIR: /tmp` to the workflow-level
   `env:` block with a comment giving the reason. No job overrides
   `TMPDIR`. Test: actionlint clean; the PR's Race and FIPS shards pass.

## Out of scope

- Shortening socket directories in the 100+ tests.
- The one-off FIPS `crypto/hmac` short-key panic in
  `TestTPMSimulator_ActivateCredentialRefusesAForeignCredential`.
