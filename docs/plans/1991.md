# Plan #1991: remove the unused local role store (SPEC row 113)

## Objective

Roles live in go-qrdb. The local role store has no reader: `UserService`
keeps a `roleDAO` field nothing reads, and the service registry fetches the
role DAO only to hand it to `NewUserService`. Remove the role service, the
role DAOs and the `RoleDAO` member of the datastore `Factory` interface.

## Slices

1. `test(...)`: delete the role tests (service `role_test.go`, quicbaas
   `role_test.go` and `role_unit_test.go`) and the role cases in the
   factory, registry, user and adapter tests.
2. `fix(...)`: delete `datastore.RoleDAO` and `Factory.RoleDAO()`,
   `pkg/platform/service/role.go`, the kvstore and quicbaas role DAOs and
   their factory methods; drop the `roleDAO` field and argument from
   `UserService` / `NewUserService` and the registry wiring.

Acceptance: no `RoleDAO` or `RoleService` left under `pkg/`; `go build
./...` passes; unit tests of the service, quicbaas, datastore and v1
webservice packages pass.

## Out of scope

- `entities.Role` itself (still used by other code).
- go.mod, go.sum, workflows; any other row.
