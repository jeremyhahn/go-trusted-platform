# Plan: #966 sleep removal, slice 9 of 9 (tenant-management and webauthn-flow e2e)

## Objective

No test at any tier sleeps. The Go tiers have no `time.Sleep` left on main;
the last sleeps are Playwright `waitForTimeout` calls in
`web/e2e/tenant-management.spec.ts` and `web/e2e/webauthn-flow.spec.ts`.
Each becomes a bounded wait on what the page renders or requests.

## Slices (this branch)

1. `tenant-management.spec.ts`
   - quota fetch error: the 500 ms sleep becomes a bounded (5 s) `waitFor`
     on the union of the error indicators, `.catch(() => {})` so the
     graceful-degradation fallback stays.
   - invitations fetch error: the 500 ms sleep becomes a bounded (5 s)
     `waitFor` on the error text, alert or retry button, same fallback.
2. `webauthn-flow.spec.ts`
   - full login flow (skipped test): the 3 s and 5 s sleeps become
     `waitForResponse` on `registration/finish` and `login/finish`.
   - backend connection errors: the 2 s sleep becomes `waitForRequest` on
     `registration/begin` and its `response()` (null when the request fails,
     the expected no-backend case).

Tests: the two specs themselves (chromium project); `make web-typecheck`
gates the skipped test.

## Out of scope

- Application code under `web/src`.
- Other specs (slices 1-8 of the row).
