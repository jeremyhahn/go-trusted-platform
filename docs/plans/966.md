# Plan: #966 test standard (no test sleeps)

## Objective
No test at any tier sleeps. Every sleep becomes a bounded polling helper or an
assertion on what the page renders. Application code is out of scope.

## Slices (this row branch: row/966-it-web-platform)
1. `test/integration/web/platform.spec.ts`: replace its 6 `waitForTimeout`
   calls. The sleep after `searchInput.first().fill('api')` becomes
   `expect(searchInput.first()).toHaveValue('api')`; each 'wait for internal
   mock data' sleep after `goto('/platform/metrics')` becomes
   `expect(authenticatedPage.locator('main')).toBeVisible()`. Test: the
   existing specs, type-checked with `tsc --noEmit`; Playwright runs in CI
   (needs the platform backend).

## Recipe (same as the merged web/e2e slices)
- after `goto` / mock data: `expect(locator('main')).toBeVisible()` or an
  expect on the element read next;
- after `fill(x)`: `expect(input).toHaveValue(x)`;
- after opening a combobox: `toHaveAttribute('aria-expanded', 'true')`;
- after choosing / Clear: `aria-expanded` `'false'` or `toHaveValue('')`;
- after `waitForLoadState('networkidle')`: delete the sleep.
Every wait uses the bounded expect timeout; no new fixed delays.

## Out of scope
Production code; other spec files (other slices of #966).
