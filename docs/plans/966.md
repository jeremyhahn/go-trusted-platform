# Plan: row #966 — no test at any tier sleeps

## Objective

Every sleep in the test suites becomes a bounded polling helper (issue #966 (c)).
The Go tiers carry no `time.Sleep` on main (the remaining `<-time.After` waits run
inside synctest bubbles); the remaining sleeps are Playwright `waitForTimeout`
calls under `web/e2e`.

## Slices (in order)

1-3. e2esmall, e2edash, e2edev: earlier lanes, the smaller specs.
4-7. Earlier sleep slices of the remaining web/e2e specs.
8. kms, networking, platform specs (this branch, `row/966-kms-net-platform`):
   - kms.spec.ts: the row-menu sleep becomes `waitFor({ state: 'visible' })` on
     the View details item; the post-click sleep becomes `waitForURL`.
   - networking.spec.ts: the PXE fetch-error sleep becomes a bounded `waitFor`
     on the error indicators; the `tryNavigate` retry sleep is removed.
   - platform.spec.ts: the metrics-503 sleep becomes a bounded `waitFor` on the
     error indicators; the auto-refresh sleep becomes `expect.poll` on the
     health route call count.
   Tests: the three specs pass on chromium as on main; `grep -c waitForTimeout`
   prints 0 for each.
9. Final slice: whatever sleeps remain on main after slice 8.

## Out of scope

- Application code under `web/src`.
- Mock latency inside `page.route` handlers (it simulates a slow API; it is not
  a wait in the test).
