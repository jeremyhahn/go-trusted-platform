# #1992 Invitation roles come from the IAM role list

## Objective

An invitation's role must be one of the tenant's IAM roles (IAM ListRoles).
The fixed ValidRoles/IsValidRole list is removed.

## Slices

1. Handler check (done, PR #1998): `pkg/webservice/v1/invitation` handler
   validates the role through a RoleLister. Codex P1: that handler has no
   production caller.
2. Service check (this slice): `InvitationService.Create`
   (`pkg/platform/service/invitation.go`) refuses a role the tenant's IAM role
   list does not hold, and refuses when the role list cannot be read or no
   lister is wired. Every transport (REST, gRPC, QUIC, MCP) reaches Create.
   - Test: `TestInvitationService_Create_RoleFromIAMRoleList` (unlisted role
     refused, listed role accepted, list error refused, no lister refused).
   - Wiring: the daemon gives the service an adapter over the QuicBaaS IAM
     client's role list.
   - `CreateInitialAdmin` is not checked: it seeds the tenant's first admin
     before tenant roles exist.

## Out of scope

- Removing the uncalled `pkg/webservice/v1/invitation` handler package.
- Role IDs vs names: the check matches the IAM role name.
