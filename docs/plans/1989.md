# Plan: #1989 transport checks that allow by default (mTLS)

## Objective

Every call a client certificate authenticates is decided by the IAM policy
enforcement point (`IAMPEPClient.Check`) for the certificate principal and the
permission the operation needs. The wildcard `mtlsOperatorScope` grant is
removed.

## Slices

1. gRPC mTLS interceptor (this slice).
   - `MTLSInterceptorConfig` gains a `PEP` field; `selectGRPCTCPAuth` passes
     the daemon PEP in.
   - After the certificate authenticates, both interceptors look up
     `grpc.ScopeForMethod(FullMethod)`, split it into resource and action and
     call `checkPrincipalPermission` for the identity's ID and tenant.
     Not allowed, no catalog entry or a malformed scope: PermissionDenied.
     A nil PEP or a failed check: Unavailable (fail closed).
   - Skip methods keep bypassing authentication and the check.
   - Tests (mtls_interceptor_test.go):
     TestMTLSUnaryInterceptor_PrincipalWithoutMethodPermissionIsDenied,
     TestMTLSUnaryInterceptor_PrincipalWithMethodPermissionSucceeds,
     TestMTLSStreamInterceptor_PrincipalWithoutMethodPermissionIsDenied,
     TestMTLSUnaryInterceptor_NilPEPFailsClosed; the existing success tests
     use a catalogued method and an allowing fake PEP.
2. QUIC and REST peer authenticator: drop `mtlsOperatorScope` from
   `mtlsPeerAuthenticator` and decide each operation/route with the PEP;
   delete the constant.

## Out of scope

- go-quicbaas changes, module bumps, pkg/auth.
- Exporting the gRPC pre-auth list: under mTLS the pre-auth methods the
  daemon serves without a credential are the transport skip methods.
