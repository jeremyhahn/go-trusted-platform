# Plan #2008: EAB gRPC enforces the caller tenant (SPEC row 125)

## Objective

`EABGRPCService.CreateCredential`, `ListCredentials` and
`CleanupExpiredCredentials` (pkg/api/daemon/eab_grpc_service.go) took
`req.GetTenantId()` unchecked, and an empty tenant on cleanup removed the
expired credentials of every tenant. A tenant-bound caller must reach only
its own tenant: every other tenant, and the all-tenant cleanup, is refused
with `PermissionDenied` through `authorizeIAMTenant`, the rule the IAM gRPC
service already applies. An unbound (local) caller keeps today's behaviour.

## Slices

1. `test(daemon)`: `TestEABGRPCService_CallerTenantBinding` drives the three
   methods with a token caller bound to `tenant-a`: own tenant OK, another
   tenant and the empty tenant PermissionDenied, unbound caller OK.
2. `fix(daemon)`: call `authorizeIAMTenant(ctx, req.GetTenantId())` in the
   three methods before the service call.

## Out of scope

- The other EAB methods (Get, Revoke, Delete, ...) that take a credential id.
- REST, QUIC and MCP EAB surfaces; go.mod, go.sum, workflows.
