# #669: SPEC row factory-image

Row branch `row/669-factory-image`, one PR. The image itself is on main
(epic #667, PRs #677-#682): `make image-rpi`, `platformctl diskimage`, the
userspace and QEMU virt integration suites, and `docs/factory-image/`.

## Objective

The SPEC row's acceptance (docs/SPEC.md): every image, an upgrade included,
is built and signed centrally and **carries a measurement manifest**: a
signed CoRIM (draft-ietf-rats-corim-11) of the PCR values the image
determines, **registered in the go-quicbaas reference-values store**. That
manifest is the part not built. Today the image has no manifest. The repo has
no CoRIM encoder and no PCR prediction from image artefacts. The pinned
go-quicbaas v0.12.0-alpha has no reference-values verbs.

## Slices, in order

1. **Plan and status** (done): this file. user.md and
   docs/firmware/rpi.md say that the image carries no measurement manifest
   yet, and why.
2. **Manifest design** (done): `docs/factory-image/architecture.md` §8.
   It sets out the following:
   - the PCRs the image determines: 0, 4, 7 and 9, plus 11 and 1/2/5 if
     the replay proves them;
   - that runtime PCRs are never in the manifest;
   - the prediction, the CoRIM/CoMID shape, and the KMS-held signing key;
   - registration and three open questions.
   The open questions are answered before slice 4 starts. They concern the
   CoRIM, its key and registration, not the prediction.
3. **PCR prediction** (`pkg/diskimage/measure`). Three parts:
   - (3a, done) replay and the TCG event encodings, pinned to digests from
     real event logs, plus PCR 5, 7 and 9 per bank;
   - (3b, done) PCR 0 from the version string read out of the U-Boot
     binary; PCR 4 from the PE/COFF sectioned Authenticode digest of the UKI
     and of the kernel in its `.linux` section, checked against the digest
     `pkg/authenticode` signs; PCR 11 from the UKI sections in
     systemd-stub's order. A UKI whose measurement the stub chooses at boot
     (`.profile`, `.dtbauto`, `.hwids`, `.efifw`) is refused;
   - (3c, done) `measure.Predict` gives PCRs 0, 4, 7 and 9 for every
     bank (sha256 when none is given) from one `measure.Image`: U-Boot's
     version, the UKI and its kernel, PK/KEK/db/dbx and the db authority,
     and the `.initrd` with the `.cmdline` as systemd-stub's load options.
     `measure.ReadImage` reads that image from the build's files: u-boot.bin,
     the UKI, `PK.crt`/`KEK.crt`/`DB.crt`, rebuilt as the signature lists
     `mkesl.py` preseeds under U-Boot's owner GUID. A missing input fails it;
   - (3d, done) `diskimage.Create` copies the signed UKI out of the
     finished image's EFI partition, reads U-Boot's owner GUID from its
     build manifest and predicts `BuildConfig.MeasurementBanks` (sha256 by
     default) into `Result.Measurements`; a missing input fails the build
     with a `MeasureError`. A build with Secure Boot off has no PCR 7 to
     predict: it gets no measurements and `Result.Unmeasured` says so.
   Test: the unit tests fix each PCR's value for the `diskimagetest`
   fixtures.
4. **CoRIM encode and sign**: write `measurements.corim` (COSE_Sign1) next
   to `manifest.json` / `SHA256SUMS` on every image and upgrade build. Tests:
   encode/decode round trip, a signature check, a refusal on a missing
   input.
   - (4a, done) `measure.EncodeManifest` / `DecodeManifest`: the
     `tagged-unsigned-corim-map` (#6.501) holding one CoMID (#6.506) in
     §8's shape, deterministic CBOR, PCR-keyed measurements with one Named
     Information digest per bank; the tag identity is `ManifestTagID`, a
     version 8 UUID from the image's SHA-256. An incomplete manifest, a
     runtime PCR or an unknown bank is refused both ways;
   - (4b, done) `measure.SignManifest` / `VerifyManifest`: the
     `signed-corim` (#6.18 COSE_Sign1) over that encoding, with alg,
     content type, kid, `corim-meta` and `x5chain` protected. The algorithm
     follows the key (ES256/ES384/ES512, PS256), so §8's open question 2
     picks the key and needs no code change. Verification refuses a bad
     signature, chain, kid, algorithm, content type or validity window;
   - (4c) the `kms://` signer resolved through the platform client, and
     writing `measurements.corim` from `Create`.
5. **Event-log reproduction**: `make integration-test-diskimage` checks that
   the manifest's values equal the live TCG event log replay on the QEMU
   virt aarch64 stand-in. The x86 ISO path gets the same check.
6. **Registration**: `platformctl diskimage` registers the manifest through
   `Security().Attestation().PutReferenceValues`. Blocked: that verb ships
   in go-quicbaas v0.14.0-alpha, and only v0.12.0-alpha is pinned and
   fetchable (#720, #906/#937, go-xkms #1443).
7. **Hardware validation** (operator): a Pi 4 with the SLB9670 follows
   user.md's runbook and attests. The operator does this on the device.
8. The row PR marks `factory-image` shipped in docs/SPEC.md.

## Out of scope

Pi OTP signed boot, A/B partitions, OTA (docs/firmware/rpi.md stays
PLANNED), Pi 5 beyond the experimental model entry, firstboot LUKS re-key.
