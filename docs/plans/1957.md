# Plan: #1957 SDK REST IAM apps

## Objective

The Go SDK REST transport reaches the six IAM app RPCs (RegisterApp, ListApps,
GetApp, UpdateApp, DeleteApp, RotateAppSecret) over the tenant-scoped app
routes, and the production IAM adapter behind those routes returns what the
SDK promises.

## Slices, in order

1. SDK routes and decoders for the six app RPCs (sdk/go/transport/rest/iam_apps.go).
   Tests: `TestAppMethodsOverSDKREST_RoundTrip` (pkg/webservice/v1/rest/iam/app_sdk_test.go)
   and the transport's `IAMApp` tests.
2. ListApps sends a non-default page as limit and offset.
   Test: the paging step of `TestAppMethodsOverSDKREST_RoundTrip`.
3. Codex round 2 (PR #1963): the production adapter keeps ResourcePrefix and
   ResponseTypes on register and update and returns both, pages the upstream
   list and fills Total, and names the rotated app (Get, then RotateSecret).
   Test: `TestAppMethodsOverSDKREST_ProductionAdapter` drives
   `NewQuicBaaSIAMAdapter` over a controlled upstream; `TestCov964Adapter_RotateAppSecret`
   expects the fetched name.

## Out of scope

- Daemon gRPC IAM app methods and `AppToProto`.
- Other upstream app fields (Description, Scopes, Contacts and the rest).
- go.mod and go.sum changes; workflows.
