# Plan: #1697 gateway-supervisor sd_notify readiness

Objective: give `pkg/sdn/gateway/process` the sd_notify(3) pieces that
`docs/gateway/supervisor.md` (c) "sd_notify-style readiness and health"
requires: a parser for the wire format, a per-child `NOTIFY_SOCKET` that only
accepts datagrams whose `SCM_CREDENTIALS` pid is the child's, and the
child-side sender. All of it builds on the pinned lower releases; go.mod does
not change.

## Slices, in order

1. Parser, socket and sender (this lane).
   - `notify.go`: `ParseNotify([]byte) (NotifyMessage, error)` for `READY=1`,
     `STATUS=`, `WATCHDOG=1`, `STOPPING=1`, `ERRNO=`; unknown keys are kept,
     not refused (sd_notify(3) ignores them); `NotifySocket` (AF_UNIX
     SOCK_DGRAM, SO_PASSCRED, drops a datagram whose pid is not the expected
     child's); `Notify(state string) error` reading `NOTIFY_SOCKET`, a no-op
     when it is unset.
   - `errors.go`: typed `ErrNotifyEmpty`, `ErrNotifyMalformed`,
     `ErrNotifySocket`, `ErrNotifySend`.
   - Tests: `TestParseNotify_*` (each key, malformed and unknown lines),
     `TestNotifySocket_RefusesForeignPid`, `TestNotifySocket_AcceptsChildReady`,
     `TestNotify_NoSocketIsNoop`; none needs root.
2. Wiring (later lane): the supervisor creates one socket per child in its
   runtime directory, passes `NOTIFY_SOCKET` and `WATCHDOG_USEC`, and
   replaces the readiness polling loop with `READY=1`, under
   `DefaultReadyTimeout`.
3. Watchdog and reporting (later lane): missed `WATCHDOG=1` deadlines are
   failures under the restart policy; `STOPPING=1` marks an orderly exit;
   `STATUS=` and `ERRNO=` are carried into the service report.

## Out of scope for slice 1

- Any change to the supervisor's polling loop or launcher environment.
- Child services sending `READY=1` (they adopt `Notify` with the wiring).
- File-descriptor store messages (`FDSTORE=1`) and `MAINPID=`.
