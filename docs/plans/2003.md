# Plan: #2003, SDN policy reads the device tenant from the subject OU (SPEC row 122)

## Objective

The device tenant is the certificate subject OrganizationalUnit, and only
that. A certificate without a non-empty OU has no tenant: the policy
extractor returns `ErrMissingTenantID`, and the enrollment hook refuses to
build a port policy, so the device gets no port assignment. Today
`X509PolicyExtractor.ExtractTenantID` falls back to O and then the first CN
label, and the enrollment hook uses O or the literal "default".

## Slices

1. `test(sdn/policy)`: cases in `certificate_test.go` (OU wins over O and
   CN; O only, CN only and an empty OU give `ErrMissingTenantID`) and in
   `enrollment_hook_test.go` (OU is the policy tenant; an O-only or CN-only
   certificate builds no policy and `PrecheckActivation` refuses with no
   `ApplyPolicy` call). The old "default tenant" coverage case now expects
   the refusal.
2. `fix(sdn/policy)`: `ExtractTenantID` reads the first OU only; the
   enrollment hook calls it (through its extractor, or a plain
   `X509PolicyExtractor` when none is configured) and returns the error
   instead of "default". Test fixtures that relied on O as the tenant carry
   the same value as OU.

## Out of scope

- Issuing OU in device certificates (CA side), go.mod/go.sum, workflows.
- Any other SPEC row.
